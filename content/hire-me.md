# Hire me

I’m genuinely busy and only open to new collaborations if (beyond compensation) they meet these baseline criteria:

- Senior-level or consistently high-quality engineering standards across the team.
- Top-tier designers with a proven track record in enterprise web products and web apps, plus a real understanding of the web’s technical constraints and possibilities.
- Long-term projects (5+ years). No disposable landing pages or short-lived marketing campaigns.
- Strong commitment to best practices. No cutting corners. Scalability and deep project understanding come first.

Bonus points:

- HQ or main office in Los Angeles.
- Team mostly operating in PT or CT hours.
- Small, focused team (~7 people total: designers, devs, copy, PM).
