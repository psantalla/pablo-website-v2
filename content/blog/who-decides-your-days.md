The only real security is the one you build yourself. Anything an arrangement hands you can be taken back the moment the arrangement changes, so security that depends on someone else was never quite yours to begin with. Building your own doesn't have to mean starting a company, though that is the fullest version of the idea. What it asks for is ownership: treating the outcome as yours, all of it, including the parts that don't feel like your fault.

Take the extreme case. You get let go for reasons well outside your control, and it is still yours to answer for, because you are the one who lives with what comes next. Once you hold it that way, the same setback starts to move you toward positions you would never reach while you let the company you work for decide your future.

So it isn't about working longer, or about being better at the work. It's about who gets to decide what your days look like.
