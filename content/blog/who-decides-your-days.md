The only real security is the one you build yourself, not the kind an arrangement hands you, because anything an arrangement gives it can take back the moment it changes. That doesn't have to mean starting a company. A company is the fullest version of the idea, not the requirement. The requirement is ownership: you treat the outcome as yours, all of it, including the parts that don't feel like your fault.

Take the extreme case. You get let go for reasons well outside your control, and it is still yours to answer for, because you own what comes next. Seen that way, the same setback moves you toward positions you would never reach while you let the company you work for decide your future.

It isn't about working longer, or even about being better at the work. It's about who gets to decide what your days look like.
