There's a kind of security no arrangement can give you, because anything granted can be taken back the moment the arrangement changes. The only version that holds is the one you build yourself. A company is the fullest expression of that, not a requirement for it. What it really asks is that you own the outcome, all of it, including the parts that don't feel like yours.

In the extreme case you get let go for reasons that sit galaxies outside your control, and it is still yours to carry: you own what comes next. That shift moves you toward positions you would never reach while you let the group you work for decide how things go. It isn't about working longer, or even about being better at the work. It's about who gets to decide what your days look like.
