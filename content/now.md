We’re aiming to launch the new platform in Q1 next year, and I think it’s going to be really strong.

I’m starting to modernize my old blueprint **Docupatient**, with the idea of laying solid foundations now and teaming up with a top designer later next year to launch it as a simple, polished, and easy-to-use SaaS.

I really enjoy projects in health and science. I’ve worked with several clinics and physiotherapists, and I’ve got the whole system very well covered: doctor schedules, patient bookings, private files, and test results. Now feels like the right time to take that next step.
